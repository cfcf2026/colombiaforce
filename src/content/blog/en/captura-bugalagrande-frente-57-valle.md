The National Police, working with the Army, Air Force and Attorney General's Office, arrested nine alleged members of Front 57 "Yair Bermúdez," a FARC dissident faction aligned with the Coordinadora Nacional Ejército Bolivariano, in Bugalagrande, Valle del Cauca.

Among those arrested were alias "Majiño," identified as the leader of a local cell and also listed on Nariño department's most-wanted list, and alias "Bastidas."

The operation seized 12 cell phones, a laptop, nine containers of black powder, and a homemade mortar grenade adapted for drone delivery.

*Figures and claims are those reported by official sources and have not been independently verified. People who have been arrested or named are presumed innocent until a judge rules otherwise.*
