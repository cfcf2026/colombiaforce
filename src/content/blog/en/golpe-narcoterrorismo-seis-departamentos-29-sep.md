President Abelardo De La Espriella confirmed the arrest of 30 people allegedly linked to narco-terrorist organizations, in a simultaneous offensive carried out in Caquetá, Boyacá, Risaralda, Cesar, Putumayo and Huila.

## Arrests by department

- **Caquetá:** 7 members of the Rodrigo Cadete structure (FARC dissidents), in La Montañita.
- **Boyacá:** 2 ELN members, in Chita.
- **Risaralda:** 15 members of "Los Fantasmas," an organized crime group, in La Virginia.
- **Cesar and Putumayo:** 6 arrests in an operation against fuel theft in Aguachica (Cesar) and Orito (Putumayo), recovering 10,500 gallons of crude diverted from the San Miguel–Orito pipeline.

## Other results

In Teruel, Huila, authorities destroyed an illegal cache belonging to the Ismael Ruiz structure (FARC dissidents) containing 36 homemade grenades and explosive material. In Campoalegre, also in Huila, police seized 149 kg of marijuana.

*Figures and claims are those reported by official sources and have not been independently verified. People who have been arrested or named are presumed innocent until a judge rules otherwise.*
