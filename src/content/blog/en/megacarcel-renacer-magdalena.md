The national government said construction of the Renacer prison complex is moving forward in Sabanas de San Ángel, Magdalena, a project that has been awaited for years. According to Infopresidencia, President Abelardo de la Espriella ordered that its completion be sped up so it can begin operating this year.

## What is known about the project

- **Capacity:** 1,974 places.
- **Size:** about 60,000 square meters of infrastructure.
- **Progress:** around 86% physical completion, according to the Magdalena governor's office.
- **Government target:** finish the works during 2026.
- **Security:** the complex will have technology and security systems to restrict unauthorized communications from inside.

## What the government says

The executive's order, according to the statement, is that authorities stay in control of prisons, that the law be enforced and that there be no room for privileges inside detention centers.
