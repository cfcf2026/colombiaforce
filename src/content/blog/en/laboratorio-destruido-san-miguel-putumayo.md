In San Miguel, Putumayo, Army troops destroyed a cocaine-processing lab that President Abelardo De La Espriella said belonged to the Comandos de Frontera (E48).

The action was part of two anti-narcotics operations reported by the president, carried out in Putumayo and the central Pacific region, which together resulted in three arrests and drug seizures.

*Figures and claims are those reported by official sources and have not been independently verified. People who have been arrested or named are presumed innocent until a judge rules otherwise.*
