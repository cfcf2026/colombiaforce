Colombia's Army, in a joint operation with the Navy and the Air Force and with support from the tax and customs agency DIAN, seized 10 tons of coltan in Vichada, President Abelardo De La Espriella reported.

## What the government reported

- **Mineral:** 10 tons of coltan.
- **Ownership:** according to the president, it allegedly belonged to the ELN's José Daniel Pérez Carreño structure.
- **Intended destination:** the mineral was allegedly to be smuggled out of the country to Europe and Asia.
- **Estimated value:** more than 16,000 million pesos, per the president.
- **Operation:** the government said it involved several days of military intelligence infiltration and surveillance.

## Why it matters

The government presents the seizure as a blow to the ELN's finances. The president said the money would have funded weapons, explosives and drones. Coltan is an industrial mineral whose illegal mining and trade authorities have linked to armed groups.
