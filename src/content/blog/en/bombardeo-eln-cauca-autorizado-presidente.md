**DEVELOPING.** This report will be updated when the government publishes the official results of the operation.

President Abelardo De La Espriella said he authorized, at 1:00 a.m., an airstrike against ELN structures in the department of Cauca. He announced it in a video posted on his social media.

![Image from the video posted by President De La Espriella on October 22](/images/presidenteoct22.png)

## What the president said

- That the operation was authorized at 1:00 a.m. and targets ELN structures, which he called narco-terrorist.
- That security forces have instructions to act "with full force and within the framework of the law."
- That he will release the results of the operation later.

## What is still unconfirmed

So far the government has not published a tally of the operation: there are no results, exact location or participating units. This report will be updated once an official account is available.

*Figures and claims are those reported by official sources and have not been independently verified.*
