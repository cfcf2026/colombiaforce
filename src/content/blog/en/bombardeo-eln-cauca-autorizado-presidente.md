**UPDATED with the results reported by the government.**

President Abelardo De La Espriella reported that an airstrike by the armed forces in rural El Tambo, Cauca, left 9 ELN members dead. Among them, according to a preliminary identification, was alias "Marcos Pacífico," whose identity is pending fingerprint confirmation.

The operation had been announced by the president hours earlier, when he said he authorized it at 1:00 a.m.

![Image of the operation in El Tambo, Cauca](/images/bombing.png)

## What the president reported

- **Result:** 9 ELN members killed in El Tambo, Cauca.
- **Target:** alias "Marcos Pacífico," described as the second-in-command and military chief of the ELN's Southwestern War Front, and top leader of the José María Becerra front.
- **Structure:** according to the government, he led three fronts operating in Cauca, Nariño and Valle del Cauca.
- **Allegations:** the government links him to kidnappings, attacks and assaults on security forces in Cali and Buenaventura, and to orders given to ELN urban militias during the 2022 protests.
- **Identification status:** preliminary, pending fingerprint confirmation.

![Image of the operation in El Tambo, Cauca](/images/bombing1.png)

## Aircraft and timing (not officially confirmed)

According to information Colombia Force obtained from a source in the Colombian Air Force, which has not been officially confirmed, at least 5 Super Tucano aircraft, 2 Kfir fighter jets and at least 4 Black Hawk helicopters, among other assets, took part in the operation. The same source says it concluded around 3:00 a.m.

## What is still unconfirmed

The government has not yet published an official report detailing the units involved, nor the fingerprint confirmation of alias "Marcos Pacífico." This report will be updated when new official information is available.

*Figures and claims are those reported by official sources and have not been independently verified.*
