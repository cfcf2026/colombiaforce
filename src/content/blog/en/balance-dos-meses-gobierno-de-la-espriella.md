President Abelardo De La Espriella delivered a presidential address presenting a security balance for his first two months in office, since taking office on August 7, 2026.

## Arrests and seizures

According to the figures presented, the security forces made **32,503 arrests** nationwide (27,802 in flagrante delicto and 4,701 under judicial order). On drug trafficking, authorities reported seizing 125,182 kg of cocaine, 60,101 kg of marijuana, 14,607 kg of coca paste and base, and 1,092 kg of heroin, for a total of over 200,000 kg. Authorities also seized 3,456 firearms and destroyed or disabled 679 pieces of infrastructure used to process drugs.

## Blows against armed structures

The president said 33 leaders of narco-terrorist organizations have been affected, including alias "La Morocha" (Rodrigo Cadete), alias "Lobo" (Structure 36), and alias "Maxmax," identified as an explosives expert for the Jaime Martínez structure. In the specific balance against Organized Armed Groups and Organized Criminal Groups, authorities reported 1,117 actions against members, 876 arrests, 70 killed in operations, and 171 demobilizations or surrenders.

*Figures and claims are those reported by official sources and have not been independently verified. People who have been arrested or named are presumed innocent until a judge rules otherwise.*
