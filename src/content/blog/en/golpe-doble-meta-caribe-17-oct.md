The National Army, through Task Force Omega, carried out Operation Decisiva in Puerto Rico, Meta, against the Marco Aurelio Buendía narco-terrorist structure, a FARC dissident faction led by alias Calarcá.

## Results in Meta

![Seized material from Operation Decisiva, Puerto Rico, Meta](/images/ejercito.png)

One structure member was killed during military action and three people were arrested, including alias "Junior Rojas," wounded during the operation and identified as second-in-command and the front's financial manager, responsible for extortion against merchants and business owners and for controlling drug production in Vistahermosa and Puerto Rico. The operation seized 2 handguns, 2 long guns, 274 rounds of ammunition, 4 magazines, a telescopic sight, and 3 hand grenades.

## Interception in the Caribbean

In the Caribbean Sea, 129 nautical miles west of Cartagena, the Navy, with support from the Air Force and in coordination with the Attorney General's Office, intercepted a go-fast vessel. Five alleged traffickers were arrested and 3,688 kg of cocaine, valued at about US$170 million, were seized along with the boat.
